## 2026-08-01 — Cycle 1: collection

- Collected **18 new sources** across all five themes (status: `collected`, pending review due 2026-08-04).
  - Consumer behaviour: 3 · Brand strategy: 4 · Retailer & fulfilment: 4 · Technology & rails: 4 · Regulatory & trust: 3
- **Notable findings:**
  - **Accenture 2026 Consumer Pulse** (25,590 consumers, 16 countries): 74% would trust a personal AI agent more than their best friend to make a purchase — but 37% of loyal shoppers would still let the agent switch brands for a better fit, making loyalty conditional rather than locked.
  - **McKinsey Europe**: 84% of European consumers use AI daily; the region operates on a 'conditional delegation' model (decision support yes, full autonomy no), aligning with EU AI Act / PSD3 trust norms.
  - **Forrester mid-2026**: most current 'agentic' commerce experiences are still assistive, not autonomous — but answer engines are already the primary discovery surface, and that traffic converts exceptionally well.
  - **Shopify Spring 2026 Edition / Shopify Catalog**: AI searches via Catalog convert at 2x the rate of scraped data — the clearest empirical case yet for structured-data availability replacing shelf placement.
  - **Adobe Digital Insights**: AI-sourced traffic to US retail sites up 393% YoY in Q1 2026, converting 42% better than non-AI traffic — but most retail sites are not machine-readable enough to capture it.
  - **Salesforce Agentforce Commerce GA (July 6, 2026)**: consumer reliance on AI as the first shopping stop grew 200% May 2025 → May 2026; retailers running their own shopper agents grew sales 59% faster.
  - **US AI AGENT Act (Sen. Warner, June 2026)**: first serious US federal proposal to regulate consumer-facing agentic AI, requiring non-discriminatory platform access for vetted third-party agents.
  - **Mastercard Agent Pay for Machines (June 10, 2026)**: machine-to-machine payments at fractions-of-a-cent scale across 30+ partners; IMF published parallel framework (April 2026) warning of irreversible-payment risk without adequate authorisation controls.
  - **Amazon**: merged Rufus + Alexa+ into 'Alexa for Shopping' (May 2026); Buy for Me feature sparked retailer backlash from brands receiving agent-initiated orders without opting in.
  - **Deloitte B2B**: 40% of B2B buyers already use agentic AI in purchasing; suppliers lag at 24% adoption. Confirms B2B as the clearest domain where structured-data availability was always decisive.
- Priority publishers found: McKinsey (EU report), Deloitte (APAC + B2B), Accenture (Consumer Pulse), Gartner (Hype Cycle), Forrester (Mid-2026 state), Salesforce, Mastercard, Visa, Adobe, Shopify (Spring Edition), IMF.
