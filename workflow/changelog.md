### Notable findings this cycle
- **Accenture "Talk to My AI Agent" (June 2026):** 74% of consumers would trust an AI agent more than their best friend to make a purchase; introduces "delegation dial" concept showing trust is category-specific, not universal.
- **Adobe Analytics Q1 2026:** AI-referred traffic to US retail sites up 393% YoY; AI traffic now converts 42% *better* than human traffic—an 80-percentage-point swing from March 2025 when it converted 38% worse.
- **HM Treasury consultation (July 2026):** UK proposes mandatory "Know Your Agent" standard and overhaul of Payment Services Regulations; consultation closes 6 Oct 2026—first major Western regulatory move specifically addressing agentic payments.
- **IMF working note (April 2026):** First IMF-level analysis of how agentic AI reshapes global payment systems; warns of systemic risk from probabilistic AI behaviour meeting deterministic payment infrastructure.
- **Byron Sharp framework extensions:** Multiple 2026 sources (Fazer Agency, Search Engine Land) now explicitly propose "AI availability" as a third layer alongside physical and mental availability—the thesis of this white paper is entering mainstream marketing discourse.
- **Amazon strategic pivot:** Amazon moved from walled-garden Buy for Me to licensing its AI shopping technology to outside retailers—signals the shelf-ownership contest is still unresolved, no single platform has won.
- **Watch-source Mollie:** Mollie published a detailed European Strategy & Payments FAQ for agentic commerce—first time the watch-source produced genuinely relevant, verifiable content. **Included: tc-c1-05.**
