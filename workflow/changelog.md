### Notable findings this cycle

- **Shopify vs Amazon split** (Sep 2026): Shopify opened full checkout to browser-based AI agents (TechCrunch, Sep 28); Amazon simultaneously blocked Meta's Muse agent from its platform to protect its $68B ad business (Forbes, Sep 21). The industry is fracturing between open-access and walled-garden models of agent availability.
- **Personal Agent Protocol** (Oct 6, 2026): Meta, Sierra, Walmart, Shopify, Stripe and 35+ partners published a draft open standard (PAP) for how personal AI agents access brand and merchant data. OpenAI joined as design partner Oct 9. This is the first attempt to formally codify the rules of agent-era availability.
- **Mastercard Agent Pay Trust Framework** (Sep 30, 2026): AI-initiated transactions now get a probability score; Cloudflare and Skyfire (Know-Your-Agent) are the intelligence partners. Trust verification is becoming a gated prerequisite for payment-rail physical availability.
- **Visa + OpenAI** (Jun 10, 2026): Visa embedded its payment network, tokenized credentials, and fraud infrastructure into OpenAI experiences. User-defined spending limits and merchant restrictions define a new consumer-agent trust model.
- **Mastercard AP4M** (Jun 10, 2026): Agent-to-agent and machine-to-machine transactions across cards, banks, and stablecoins. Microtransaction support signals a plane of commerce entirely outside human brand-salience.
- **Bain brand strategy** (May 2026): Two Bain briefs — "When People Never See Your App" and "Rewiring Demand Generation" — crystallise the Sharp displacement thesis commercially: brands must design for agent legibility, not human experience.
- **Gartner Hype Cycle for Agentic AI** (Apr 2026): First Gartner Hype Cycle dedicated to agentic AI places the category at Peak of Inflated Expectations; governance, security, and cost profiles rising. Only 17% of orgs have deployed agents.
- **Gartner: AI agents to outnumber sellers 10:1 by 2028** (Jul 2026): Selling agents will vastly outnumber humans, eliminating the human-relationship channel through which brand salience used to operate in B2B.
- **Adobe traffic data** (Aug 2026): AI-referred retail traffic up 393% YoY in Q1 2026; AI visitors convert at 60% higher rate. Quantifies the premium for being machine-readable and agent-discoverable.
- **Forrester reality check** (mid-2026): Confirms most 'agentic' experiences are still conversational discovery; humans still own checkout in most cases. Useful counterweight to hype.
- **CERRE & UK CMA** (Jul + Mar 2026): European and UK regulators formalising consumer-law frameworks for agentic AI; EU consumer law's human-buyer assumption is the regulatory mirror of Sharp's human-buyer assumption.
- **Deloitte B2B** (Jun 2026): 38% of B2B buyers already use agentic AI in purchasing vs 24% of suppliers — buyer adoption racing ahead of seller readiness.
- **McKinsey Europe** (Mar 2026): 84% of EU consumers use AI daily; 38% use it in shopping, primarily at discovery/comparison stage, not checkout. Maps current Sharp displacement zone geographically.
